## 2. The proven rule (the heart of this slice)

```
bill.estimatedNextFundingAmount = ceil( bill.amount * interval_days / 30.4375 )
```

`interval_days` = the funding plan's cadence in days (**14** here: Crew reports `frequency WEEKLY, frequencyInterval 2`, which `meridian/providers/crewwork.py:46` already maps to `"biweekly"`). `30.4375 = 365.25/12`. The residual is **positive on every bill**, so it is a ceiling, not a round — rounding to nearest misses two of the five.

Verified in cents against Crew's own payload, all five bills:

| bill | `amount` | Crew's `estimatedNextFundingAmount` | `amount × 14 / 30.4375` | ceil |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rent | 144200 | 66327 | 66326.1 | **66327** |
| Verizon Payment Arrangement | 7520 | 3459 | 3458.9 | **3459** |
| Verizon | 10157 | 4672 | 4671.8 | **4672** |
| Eversource | 21000 | 9660 | 9659.1 | **9660** |
| Xfinity | 9300 | 4278 | 4277.6 | **4278** |

**Meaning:** every monthly bill accrues a daily-rate share of itself from **every** funding event, so it is funded by its due date. That is the Simple/Beacon mechanic the owner named — Crew already does it. A bill's contribution depends only on its own amount, the interval and the month length; it does **not** change when another bill joins the reserve.

Crew also reports two fields Meridian currently discards, which are the schedule's skeleton:

- **`reservedBy`** (per bill) — the deadline that reservation is for; observed to be exactly that bill's next due date: `2026-09-22` (Verizon), `2026-09-30` (Eversource, Xfinity), `2026-10-16` (Rent, Verizon Payment Arrangement).
- **`nextFundingDate`** (per reserve) — `2026-10-02`, the plan's own next event (anchor `2026-09-04` + 2 × 14d).

Other raw facts worth keeping: each bill is `status: ACTIVATED`, `paused: false`, `autoAdjustAmount: true`, `frequency: MONTHLY`, `frequencyInterval: 1`, with `dayOfMonth` 16/16/22/30/30 and `anchorDate` `2026-09-16` / `2026-01-16` / `2026-01-22` / `2026-01-30` / `2026-01-30`. Rent and Verizon Payment Arrangement carry `daysOverdue: 3` (their `2026-09-16` occurrence has passed). The reserve funds from a Checking subaccount (`reserve.settings.funding.subaccount`).

**Falsified on real data** (recorded so nobody re-proposes them): an even split (`109710/5 = 21942` cents is not observed), a proportional split, and a nearest-due-first waterfall (Eversource and Xfinity are due `09-30` and hold `0`, while Rent is due `10-16` and holds all `109710`).

## 3. Decisions to make before writing code

**Decision 1 — compute, or ingest?** Recommendation: **compute first, ingest second.**
- *(A) Compute-only, no migration.* Everything the schedule needs is already stored: `commitments.amount`, `due_date`, `recurrence`, `bill_reserve_id`; `crew_funding_plans.cadence` + `anchor_date`; the month constant. Basis would be `"projected"` — *Meridian applying Crew's published rule*, which must be worded exactly that way and never as "Crew says".
- *(B) Ingest Crew's reported `estimatedNextFundingAmount` / `reservedBy` / `nextFundingDate`.* Stronger provenance — those are Crew's own statements — and it enables a **divergence test** (computed vs reported), which is the best correctness evidence available. Cost: a new migration (025) plus `CommitmentCandidate` / `NormalizedBillReserve` / repository / both mapping sites, exactly the OS-048a/048b pattern.
- Doing (A) alone is a smaller, honest, owner-visible slice. Doing (B) without (A) leaves nothing to display. If context allows, do (A) then (B) as two commits in one slice; if not, do (A) and file (B).

**Decision 2 — horizon.** Today's horizon is `2026-09-19 → 2026-10-03`, so **Rent (next due `2026-10-16`, the only bill with money set aside) is invisible** and the most interesting line is off-screen. Recommend extending the horizon to cover at least through the next `reservedBy` date, or adding the schedule to the row for the bill's own next occurrence regardless of the horizon — owner-facing choice, not yours.

**Decision 3 — the unit is the bill's NEXT occurrence, not the schedule.** Crew states one `reservedBy` per bill. Do **not** multiply a contribution across future occurrences: state the schedule toward the next deadline only. This preserves D-010's prohibition as narrowed by D-013 and keeps the dial from implying funded future cycles.

**Decision 4 — retirement of the `derived` branch (OS-055).** D-015 retires the even split as a *model*. Recommend removing or explicitly gating the derived branch in this slice so only one projection vocabulary exists (Crew's proration), rather than leaving two contradictory mechanisms.
